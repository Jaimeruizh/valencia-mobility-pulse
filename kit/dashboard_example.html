Where the numbers come from

  • Bike availability: ValenBisi Disponibilidad, refreshed by the city every 10 minutes.
  • Traffic: Intensidad tráfico tramos. The portal publishes the latest sensor reading per street segment and does not state its unit.
  • Neighbourhood outlines: Barrios. Areas are calculated from the outlines.
  • Bus stops: EMT. Cycle routes: Itinerarios ciclistas, each stretch assigned to the neighbourhood of its midpoint.
  • Vulnerability: Vulnerabilidad por barrios 2021, published for 70 of the 88 neighbourhoods.

How it stays current

The city portal only publishes the present moment. A small script saves a copy every 10 minutes, which is what makes the history charts possible. All times are València local time. Source: Ajuntament de València open data portal.
